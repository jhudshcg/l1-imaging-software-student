Learn a little. Try it. Check it. Make something that speaks to your reader.

Your challenge

Create a poster that helps someone aged 16+ understand what they could make or learn on Kickstart, your course. Combine an image of your own work with somebody else’s work and your own short text.

Make a noticeboard version and a smaller screen version. Your teacher confirms the final details before you start.

Get these details from your teacher

  • Two accurate facts about the course.
  • An approved next step or contact route.
  • Any college branding rules and image permissions.
  • The class hand-in location and date.

Do not invent course dates, fees or contact details.

Make it your own

  • Choose your layout, colour choices and image positions.
  • Keep the work recognisable and the words readable.
  • Use named layers, a group, a selection and an editable mask.
  • Create a brush detail and use a suitable Smart Filter.
  • Use feedback and Photoshop checks to improve the poster.

Complete your planning and source records in the booklet. There is no extra planning form.
