Explore a poster layout

Learn a little. Try it. Check it. Make something that speaks to your reader.

Study the layout, then choose your own

This is a layout study with labelled spaces. It is not a completed assessed poster and does not demonstrate Photoshop tools. Replace all labelled content with your own work and checked course information.

1 · Start with a clear title

The course name is the largest text. A reader can recognise the subject quickly.

2 · Let the work do some talking

Two image areas make space for different examples. Your images can have different sizes or shapes.

3 · Keep facts short

Two small sections support the images. Use only facts checked by your teacher.

4 · Give the reader a next step

The contrasting box makes the action easy to find. Your teacher supplies the correct route.

5 · Leave breathing room

Space separates the parts. The edges do not crowd the text.

Your design challenge

  1. Cover the example. What do you remember first?
  2. Imagine the second image is more important. How could you rearrange the poster?
  3. Sketch a different layout for your own two images.
  4. Choose one thing to make stand out. Explain your choice in Booklet Task 1.

Think before copying

Would this layout fit your images? What might become hard to read on a small screen? Make a change that solves a problem for your reader.
